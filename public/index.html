AI Visibility Tracker
Methodology

Which brands the AI engines actually recommend.

Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, and Google AI Mode the questions your buyers ask, then measure which brands their answers mention and cite. Track your position over time.

Setup

Add your brand and the search terms you want to monitor. One brand, as many terms as you like.

Deterministic scoring · no LLM judges · data via SearchApi.io

Settings

Stored locally in data/settings.json (gitignored) and sent only to SearchApi.io. Get a key at searchapi.io. Testing uses 1 request.
